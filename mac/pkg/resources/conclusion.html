HDR Switch is installed. To start it, open it from ~/Applications (the Applications folder in your home folder). It appears in the menu bar.

If your monitor isn't in HDR Switch's database, the app tells you when it starts. HDR and the color profile still work, but local dimming and brightness don't until you add it: click the menu bar icon, choose Settings…, then Detect Monitor….

The command line tool is optional. To use it, add hdrctl's folder to your PATH, then open a new Terminal window:
echo 'export PATH="$HOME/.local/bin:$PATH"' >> ~/.zshrc
