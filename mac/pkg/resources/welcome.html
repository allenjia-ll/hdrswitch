HDR Switch switches HDR, the color profile, local dimming and brightness of an external monitor from the menu bar.

This installs the app in your own Applications folder (~/Applications), and the small hdrctl command line tool in ~/.local/bin. No password is needed.

HDR Switch is an unofficial tool, not affiliated with or endorsed by any monitor's manufacturer or Apple. It uses private macOS APIs and writes settings to your monitor over DDC/CI. Use it at your own risk.
